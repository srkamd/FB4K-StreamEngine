The Direct Answer: How to Save Your Own Content

If you uploaded a video to Facebook and lost your original local file, you do not need third-party tools to recover it. Facebook provides two direct, official methods: the single-video menu download option on desktop, and the comprehensive Download Your Information (DYI) archive tool within your Account Center. Both methods give you clean, official MP4 copies without watermarks or quality tampering.

Method 1: Downloading Individual Videos from Your Profile

For videos you previously published on your personal timeline or page, follow this quick numbered process:

  1. Open Facebook on a desktop browser and navigate to your profile page.
  2. Click on the Videos tab beneath your cover photo, then select Your Videos.
  3. Click on the video thumbnail to open the full video player view.
  4. Click the three-dot menu icon (•••) in the top right corner of the post.
  5. Select Download Video. The native MP4 file will immediately download to your computer.
Note for Mobile App Users: The native Facebook mobile app currently omits the direct "Download Video" button for most timelines. To download directly on mobile, open your browser (Chrome or Safari), request Desktop Site, and access your video tab.

Method 2: Batch Exporting via "Download Your Information"

If you need to archive all your videos, Reels, and photos across several years of posting, Facebook's Account Center offers an official complete archive export:

Setting Recommended Choice Why
Date Range All Time or Custom Range Ensures full historical backup
Format JSON or HTML HTML is easiest to browse offline; JSON is ideal for developers
Media Quality High Guarantees Facebook preserves the highest resolution stored on their servers

Step-by-Step Archive Request

  1. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Accounts Center.
  2. Under Account Settings, select Your Information and Permissions.
  3. Click Download Your Information, then choose Download or transfer information.
  4. Select Specific types of information and check only Posts (which includes your videos and reels).
  5. Choose Download to device, select High media quality, and click Create Files.
  6. Facebook will process the archive and email you a secure download link within 24 to 48 hours.

Advanced Tips for Professional Creators & Page Managers

If you manage a Facebook Business Page or Creator profile with hundreds of published videos, the manual one-by-one method becomes inefficient. For page managers, Meta Business Suite provides an integrated Content Library view:

  • Log into Meta Business Suite at business.facebook.com.
  • From the left-hand navigation menu, select Content (or Published Posts).
  • Filter your posts by Videos or Reels.
  • Click on the three dots next to any published asset and select Download SD or Download HD.

Meta Business Suite typically delivers files encoded with the exact master parameters you originally provided, bypassing the heavier downstream client player re-encodes.

Handling Live Stream Broadcast Backups

Facebook Live broadcasts present unique challenges. When a live stream concludes, Facebook saves the recorded session to your profile. However, during live transmission, transient network drops can introduce packet loss or audio/video desync.

To preserve live streams in pristine quality:

  1. Wait at least 30 to 60 minutes after the broadcast ends before attempting to export. This allows Meta's servers to complete background multi-pass transcode processing and manifest stitching.
  2. Always export through the desktop interface or Meta Business Suite rather than the mobile app to ensure you receive the Full HD rendition rather than a reduced mobile stream.
  3. Store the exported file immediately into a dedicated local folder following our 3-2-1 backup strategy.

Frequently Asked Questions on Official Downloads

Question Answer
Why is the downloaded video only 720p when I uploaded 4K? Facebook automatically downsamples video ingest to 720p or 1080p to control global CDN streaming bandwidth. To understand the mechanics of this, read our guide on Facebook video resolutions explained.
How long does the Account Center archive link remain valid? Meta's generated archive download links expire after 4 days for security reasons. Download the file promptly to your local hard drive upon receiving the email notification.
Does the archive include deleted videos? No. Content that was permanently deleted before requesting the archive cannot be recovered via Account Center. Always maintain local master copies prior to deleting posts.
