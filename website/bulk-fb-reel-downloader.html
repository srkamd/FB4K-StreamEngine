The Engineering Behind Batch Media Archiving

Archiving multiple social media videos individually is a tedious, repetitive workflow. For video creators, research scholars, and digital media archivists, downloading dozens of educational lessons or campaign reels one-by-one requires continuous manual URL switching and multiple browser tab navigations. The FB4KDownloader Batch Queue solves this efficiency bottleneck by introducing a parallel client-side extraction queue that processes up to 10 video links simultaneously.

Parallel Stream Manifest Parsing

When you submit a list of links, our asynchronous queue dispatches parallel HTTP HEAD requests to analyze each post's CDN manifest. This determines video availability, native resolutions (1080p vs 720p), and duration in under 2 seconds, displaying a unified visual review table before any file transfers begin.

Zero-Disk Memory Streaming

Unlike cloud scraper services that hoard downloaded files on centralized databases, our server streams the video bytes straight through temporary in-memory RAM buffers into your browser. Your private IP address and batch queuing history are purged the moment your session completes.

Best Practices for Bulk Video Archiving

  • Ensure Link Public Visibility: Verify that each video URL has a public globe privacy icon. Group posts or private profiles will fail in batch processing.
  • One URL Per Line: Copy links into any plain text editor (.txt or .csv) or paste directly into the box with one link per line without commas or extra spaces.
  • Target Local Folder Option: On desktop browsers (Chrome, Edge), you can click Select PC Folder to save all selected videos directly into a dedicated folder without repeated browser confirmation prompts.
  • Selective Downloading: Use the individual check boxes to deselect lower-priority videos, reducing cellular data usage and speeding up download completion.

Frequently Asked Questions About Bulk Archiving

Why is the queue limited to 10 URLs per batch?

To prevent automated abusive scraping, protect Facebook server bandwidth, and ensure instantaneous response times for all users, batches are capped at 10 items. You can process unlimited successive batches.

Can I download reels and normal landscape videos in the same batch?

Yes! The queue automatically inspects each URL and applies appropriate 9:16 vertical scaling for Reels and 16:9 widescreen scaling for standard landscape posts.

Does bulk downloading compress or reduce video bitrate?

No. Every video in your queue is fetched at its highest original native master resolution (1080p or 720p) provided by Facebook's media CDN.

Official Backup Recommendations

If you are archiving large numbers of videos from your own profile or business page, we recommend using Facebook's official Account Center backup tool, which downloads your complete archive in original master quality. Read our guide on how to save your own Facebook videos officially.
