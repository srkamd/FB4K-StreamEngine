No. FB4KDownloader strictly processes public videos and reels. We respect privacy settings, closed groups, and personal friend-only posts.

2. Video Quality & 4K Upscaling

How does the 4K Ultra HD resolution option work?

Facebook streams content natively at a maximum resolution of 1080p or 720p. Our 4K option fetches the highest native quality master stream and applies high-bitrate spatial scaling (2160x3840 for 9:16 vertical reels, 3840x2160 for 16:9 landscape videos). It is clearly labeled as upscaled so you have full transparency.

What video formats are supported?

Videos are delivered in standard MP4 format (H.264/AAC), which is natively compatible with all modern smartphones, tablets, video editors (Premiere, CapCut, DaVinci), and media players (VLC, QuickTime, Windows Media Player).

3. Audio (MP3) Extraction

How do I convert a Facebook video to MP3?

When you paste the video URL, select the Audio (MP3) format card. Our server will extract the original audio stream and package it as a clean MP3 file (128kbps to 320kbps) without video track overhead.

5. Audio/Video Technical Specifications

What video and audio codecs does FB4KDownloader use?

We deliver video in universal H.264 (AVC) encoding within an MP4 container, paired with stereo AAC or MP3 audio at sample rates up to 48 kHz. This guarantees smooth playback across iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, smart TVs, and video editing suites like Adobe Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve without requiring third-party codec plugins.

Why does the 4K video file take longer to process than 1080p?

Our 4K option applies high-bitrate spatial scaling to enhance pixel sharpness and clarity for viewing on 4K computer monitors and ultra-high-definition television displays. This computationally intensive transcode is rendered on high-performance cloud servers before delivery, which requires a few extra seconds of processing time compared to progressive native streams.

Can I convert Facebook audio directly into 320 kbps MP3?

Yes. Our audio extraction engine isolates the acoustic data stream and encodes it at the industry maximum Constant Bitrate (CBR) of 320 kbps. This produces pristine acoustic clarity for musical recordings, podcasts, and university lectures while using up to 90% less device storage than the full video file.

Is there an expiration time on the downloaded video links?

Facebook's content delivery network (CDN) generates signed temporary security tokens for video streams that expire after approximately 2 to 4 hours. Once saved to your device hard drive or phone gallery, your downloaded MP4 or MP3 file is stored permanently offline and never expires.

6. Legal Rights, Copyright & Fair Use

Is it legal to download Facebook videos?

Downloading publicly accessible video content for private, non-commercial personal viewing, study, and digital archiving is widely recognized under the Fair Use doctrine (17 U.S. Code § 107). However, re-uploading, broadcasting, or commercializing content created by others without their express permission constitutes copyright infringement. Always respect original creators.

How can copyright owners request content blocking?

We honor legitimate intellectual property claims. If you are a copyright owner or authorized representative and wish to restrict public media archiving for your content, please submit a formal notice via our DMCA policy page or email copyright@fb4kdownloader.com for prompt URL blocking.
