Frequently asked questions

Is FB4KDownloader free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no registration and no subscription.

Does it work on Android and iPhone?

Yes. The site works in the browser on Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows and Mac.

Where are downloaded videos saved?

Videos go to your device's default Downloads folder. On iPhone, long-press the button and choose Download Linked File if the video plays instead.

Why did my video download in 1080p instead of 4K?

We use the best quality Facebook provides. When the original is 720p or 1080p, the 4K option is upscaled. If upscaling could cause lag on your device, we give you the native HD file instead.

Do you store my videos or links?

No. Links and temporary files are removed once your download finishes.
