How to save public Facebook videos

Three straightforward steps taking less than 15 seconds.

  1. 1

    Copy the Public Link

    On Facebook, tap Share on the video or Reel and choose Copy Link.

  2. 2

    Paste in Downloader

    Paste the link into the box above and click the Download button.

  3. 3

    Save to Your Device

    Select 1080p, 720p, or MP3. The file saves directly to your device's Downloads folder.

Frequently asked questions

Is this downloader free to use?

Yes. It is free for personal use with no account registration or hidden subscriptions.

Can I download private or closed group videos?

No. We strictly support public videos. To protect user privacy, our tool does not access password-protected accounts or closed groups. To download your own private posts, use Facebook's official Account Center backup tool.

Does it work on Android and iPhone?

Yes! It runs in any web browser on Android phones, iPhones, iPads, Windows laptops, and Mac computers.

How does the 4K resolution option work?

Facebook streams content at a maximum native resolution of 1080p or 720p. Our 4K option takes the highest native source and applies high-bitrate spatial scaling. It is clearly labeled as upscaled so you know exactly what you are getting.

Do you store copies of downloaded videos?

No. Videos are streamed directly through memory buffers to your client browser without being saved to our server hard drives.
