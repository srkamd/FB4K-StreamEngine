A complete, honest guide for saving public videos and audio across Android, iPhone, Windows, and Mac devices.

Important Notice on Content Ethics

Respect Creators' Rights: Only download videos that you own, that are in the public domain, or for which you have explicit written permission from the creator to save for personal offline use. Do not use this utility to infringe copyrights, re-upload media to your own social channels, or bypass access restrictions. Read our full copyright and legal guide.

The Quick 3-Step Process

Saving any public Facebook video or vertical Reel using FB4KDownloader takes less than fifteen seconds:

  1. Copy the Link: On the Facebook app or website, locate the public post. Tap the Share button and choose Copy Link. (On desktop, right-click the video timestamp and choose Copy link address).
  2. Paste into the Tool: Open FB4KDownloader.com in your browser. Tap the Paste button or paste the URL directly into the input box.
  3. Select Resolution & Save: Click the Download button. Choose your preferred quality format (1080p Full HD, 720p HD, 4K Upscaled, or MP3 Audio). The file will begin saving to your device immediately.

Detailed Guide for Android Users

On Android devices (Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, Xiaomi, OnePlus), downloading runs natively inside any modern browser such as Google Chrome, Firefox, or Samsung Internet:

  1. Open the Facebook app and navigate to the Reel or video you wish to save.
  2. Tap the Share icon on the bottom right and select Copy link.
  3. Open Chrome, navigate to https://fb4kdownloader.com, and paste the URL.
  4. Tap the Download button to load available streams.
  5. Tap the Full HD 1080p or HD 720p button. A system notification will appear confirming: "Downloading file...".
  6. Once finished, open your phone's Files or My Files app, tap the Downloads folder, and your video is ready for offline playback.

Detailed Guide for iPhone & iPad Users (iOS)

Apple's iOS has a built-in Safari Download Manager that makes saving media straightforward:

  1. In the Facebook app, tap Share → More Options → Copy.
  2. Open the Safari browser (we recommend Safari over Chrome on iOS for direct file saving) and visit https://fb4kdownloader.com.
  3. Paste the copied URL and tap Download.
  4. When format choices appear, tap the download button for your desired resolution.
  5. Safari will present a pop-up confirmation: "Do you want to download this file?". Tap Download.
  6. To save the video directly to your iPhone Photos / Camera Roll:
    • Tap the downward-pointing arrow in the Safari address bar (the Downloads icon).
    • Tap the downloaded video to open the preview.
    • Tap the iOS Share icon (square with upward arrow) in the bottom-left corner.
    • Select Save Video. The clip is now stored in your Camera Roll alongside your photos!

Downloading on Windows PC and Mac Computers

On desktop computers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Safari, Firefox), saving videos requires no software installation:

  • Right-click the video timestamp or click the three dots (•••) on the post and click Copy link address.
  • Paste the link into the box on our homepage.
  • Click your chosen format. The browser will save the MP4 directly into your default C:\Users\[Username]\Downloads (Windows) or /Users/[Username]/Downloads (Mac) folder.

If you download videos frequently, you can also install our official Chrome Web Store Extension, which adds an integrated download button beneath videos as you browse.

Official Facebook Alternatives (Zero Downloads)

Before using any web downloader, remember that Facebook provides two native options:

  • Save to Collection: Tap the three dots on any post and tap Save video. The video is bookmarked in your private collection at facebook.com/saved and uses 0 MB of phone storage. See our guide on how to save Facebook videos to watch later.
  • Download Your Information: If you want to recover your own historical uploads, use Facebook's official Account Center export tool. See our walkthrough on how to save your own Facebook videos officially.

Comprehensive Error Resolution & Troubleshooting Guide

While FB4KDownloader achieves a 99.4% success rate across public Facebook URLs, occasional network quirks, privacy settings, or browser cache mismatches can interrupt stream extraction. Here is our step-by-step diagnostic resolution matrix:

Issue 1: "Video stream not found or invalid URL"

Root Cause: This error occurs when the video post privacy is configured as "Friends", "Friends of Friends", or resides inside a closed Facebook Group. External web services cannot bypass Facebook's authentication firewall without user login credentials.
Solution: Open the video post in an incognito/private browser window where you are not logged in. If Facebook prompts you to sign in to see the video, the post is private. Use our Private Video Downloader page for authenticated personal media backups.

Issue 2: Mobile Safari Plays Video Instead of Downloading (iOS)

Root Cause: Apple's iOS Safari browser default behavior is to stream MP4 media in full-screen mode rather than immediately triggering a file download save prompt.
Solution: When clicking the Download button on iPhone or iPad, long-press (tap and hold) the button for 1 second until the contextual menu appears, then tap "Download Linked File". Your video will save directly into the iOS Files app inside the Downloads folder.

Issue 3: Extracted Video Has No Sound

Root Cause: Facebook uses DASH adaptive streaming, which occasionally separates high-definition 1080p video tracks from audio tracks on certain creator streams.
Solution: FB4KDownloader automatically multiplexes video and audio together on our backend. If a specific source video lacks audio, select the HD 720p or Audio only (MP3) format option, which uses Facebook's progressive combined master feed.
