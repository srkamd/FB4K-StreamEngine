Cookie & Consent Policy

How we utilize local storage, anonymous session tokens, and third-party advertising cookies under GDPR and ePrivacy regulations.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

On this page

What Are Cookies? How We Use Storage Advertising Cookies Managing Cookies GDPR & EU Consent Inquiries

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites save to your computer or mobile device when you browse pages. They allow a site to remember user actions and preferences (such as light/dark mode selection, interface state, and language) over a period of time, so you don't have to keep re-entering them whenever you navigate back.

2. How FB4KDownloader Uses Cookies & Storage

We believe in minimal telemetry. We do not track your real identity or link downloaded video URLs to any personal account. We categorize our storage mechanisms into three levels:

A. Strictly Necessary & Functional Storage (First-Party)

  • Theme Preferences (Local Storage): Stores your preferred display theme (dark or light mode) via localStorage.getItem('fb4k-theme').
  • Session Rate Limiting: In-memory ephemeral counters used by our Express server to prevent denial-of-service (DoS) spam attacks on our transcoding pipe. These contain no personal user information and expire automatically.

B. Analytics Cookies

We may monitor anonymized visitor trends (such as browser type, operating system, and geographic country) to optimize video transcode performance. Analytics data is fully aggregated and does not disclose personally identifiable information (PII).

3. Third-Party Advertising & Monetization Cookies

To keep FB4KDownloader 100% free with unlimited high-resolution downloads, we partner with reputable third-party advertising networks (including Google AdSense and CPM networks):

  • Third-party ad vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized or contextual advertisements to users.
  • These ad networks may place or recognize unique cookies on your browser to measure advertisement effectiveness.

4. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You can manage, block, or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

To opt out of personalized advertising by Google and network participants, you can visit the official Google Ads Settings and the Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out Portal.

5. EU User Consent & GDPR Compliance

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive, visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom are served cookie consent notices enabling granular opt-in/opt-out for non-essential advertising cookies.

6. Inquiries & Data Rights

If you have any questions about our Cookie Policy or how we handle your browser data, please reach out directly at privacy@fb4kdownloader.com or visit our Contact Page.
