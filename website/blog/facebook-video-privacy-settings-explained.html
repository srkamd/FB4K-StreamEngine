Facebook Video Privacy Settings Explained

Understand audience selectors: Public, Friends, Friends Except, Custom, and Group Privacy for social video publishing.

By Shahrukh Ahmad (SRK AMD)  ·  Updated October 11, 2026  ·  6 min read

The Five Core Privacy Levels Explained

Every video and Reel posted on Facebook is governed by an audience selector. The five primary privacy tiers are: Public (viewable by anyone on or off Facebook), Friends (restricted strictly to your accepted friends), Friends Except (hides from specific individuals), Specific Friends (whitelists chosen viewers), and Only Me (private personal archive).

How to Change Privacy on an Existing Video

  1. Navigate to the video on your timeline.
  2. Click the three dots (•••) in the post header.
  3. Select Edit Audience.
  4. Choose your new audience level (e.g., switch from Public to Friends) and click Done.

Privacy on Facebook Reels

Unlike regular feed posts, Reels default to Public for most creator and business accounts to maximize algorithmic distribution. If you want your Reels restricted strictly to friends, you must adjust your default Reels audience inside Settings & Privacy → Reels.

To understand why non-public links cannot be parsed by external tools, read our troubleshooting guide on why a video won't play or download.

The Difference Between Post Privacy and Account Privacy

Many creators confuse individual post privacy with their overarching account privacy settings. Even if your personal profile is locked or set to private, you can still publish individual videos with a Public audience selector. Conversely, an account with a public profile can choose to share sensitive family videos strictly with Friends or a Custom List.

Audience Optimization Checklist for Creators

  1. Professional Mode / Creator Profiles: If you want your Reels to appear in Facebook's global recommendation feed, switch your profile to Professional Mode. This ensures all new Reels default to Public.
  2. Tagging Controls: Under Settings & Privacy → Profile and Tagging, enable Review posts you're tagged in before the post appears on your profile to prevent spam tags from showing on your video feed.
  3. Location Tagging: Adding a public location tag to your video helps local audiences discover your content in Facebook Watch and Reels search.

Frequently Asked Questions and Practical Advice

Why is direct stream verification critical before local storage?

Direct verification ensures that the downloaded file contains complete video and audio tracks without corrupt moov atoms. On web servers, the moov atom contains the index of frames. If a stream disconnects prematurely, the resulting file will fail to seek or play properly.

How does bandwidth throttling affect mobile video downloads?

Cellular connections often throttle long-lived socket connections to prioritize real-time interactive traffic. To prevent download timeouts, utilize reliable Wi-Fi networks and avoid background app sleep modes when saving large video files.

What are the key ethical principles when handling third-party media?

Always obtain explicit written authorization before re-distributing, editing, or repurposing digital media. Archiving for offline study or personal reference must strictly align with statutory Fair Use principles and platform terms.
